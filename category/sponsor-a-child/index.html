Your questions

Before you sponsor.

The institute can help you understand the available options.

Are the ages shown current?

No. The profile pages label ages as recorded in the original 2019 profiles. Current ages, groups and circumstances should be confirmed with the institute.

How much does sponsorship cost?

Please ask the institute about current contributions and arrangements. No sponsorship price has been published on these pages.

Can I enquire about a particular person?

Yes. Each profile has an email enquiry button that includes the person’s name. The institute can confirm current needs and any available sponsorship arrangements.

How do I make a contribution?

Contact the institute or consult its donation page for instructions. Ask the team to confirm how your contribution will be recorded and used.
