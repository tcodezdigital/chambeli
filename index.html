Our story

Rooted in compassion.
Growing with purpose.

Founded in 1969, Chambeli Institute is a project of the Society for Mentally Handicapped Children. From our campus in Satellite Town, Rawalpindi, we provide a caring setting for children with intellectual disabilities.

Our purpose is simple: to value every person, support children’s development and help families feel supported in their journey.

Dignity for every childRespect, inclusion and opportunities to participate.
Care beyond the classroomLearning, therapy and practical everyday experiences.

Looking for support for your child or a way to contribute? Speak with our team for advice about the next step.

Call the institute ↗
How can I enquire about admission?

Call +92 51 484 2757 or email info@chambeli.org. Our team can explain the assessment process, documents, availability and arrangements relevant to your child.

Which services does Chambeli provide?

The institute’s published programs include special education, physiotherapy, speech therapy, orthotics and prosthetics, women’s fitness and vocational training. Contact us to confirm current service availability and schedules.

Can I give Zakat or sponsor a child?

Yes. The institute welcomes Zakat, Sadaqat, sponsorships and other donations. Visit the donation page for published payment instructions, or contact the team about current sponsorship needs.

Can I visit the institute?

Visitors are welcome to enquire about the institute’s work. Please contact our team before travelling to arrange a suitable visit.

Where is Chambeli Institute located?

Our published address is 20-B Satellite Town, Commercial Market, Rawalpindi, Pakistan. Call the institute if you need help with directions.
