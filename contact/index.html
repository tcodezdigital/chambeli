Helpful answers

Before you get
in touch.

We can help you identify the right department for your enquiry.

How do I enquire about school admission?

Select School / Admissions in the form, or call the institute. Ask about availability, requirements, fees and any assessment arrangements.

Can I enquire about rehabilitation or vocational training?

Yes. Tell us which service interests you so the team can explain available arrangements.

How can I help through sponsorship or donations?

Visit our sponsorship and donation pages, or contact the team to discuss current needs and contribution instructions.

What are the institute’s opening hours?

Please call before travelling to confirm current office hours and arrange your visit.
