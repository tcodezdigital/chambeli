Who we are

A long-standing purpose.
A personal approach
to care.

Chambeli Institute is a project of the Society for Mentally Handicapped Children, founded in 1969. The institute operates as a day care setting for children with intellectual disabilities, with its own campus in Satellite Town’s Commercial Market, Rawalpindi.

Our work brings together care, education and rehabilitation. Through the efforts of our staff, doctors and specialists, we seek to support each child’s development and help families feel more supported in everyday life.

At the heart of our work is a simple belief: every person deserves care, respect and recognition, without discrimination.

Our registered societySociety for Mentally Handicapped ChildrenRegistered under the Social Welfare Agencies (R&C) Ordinance 1961.Registration number: DSW/PB/70/4/19470.

What guides us

Compassion with a purpose.

Our mission, vision and objectives keep children’s wellbeing and their families at the centre of our work.

01

Our mission

To support health and wellbeing in our community, especially for children with disabilities who need dedicated care and scientific approaches to treatment.

02

Our vision

To spread happiness and help children who need support access opportunities for a better quality of life.

03

Our objectives

To care for children, support their development and help them and their parents feel happier and more at ease, while valuing every individual without discrimination.

How we support development

Learning and care.
In many meaningful forms.

Our published approach includes therapies, practical learning and shared experiences that extend beyond the classroom.

01

Physiotherapy

Physical rehabilitation forms part of the institute’s approach to care and development.

02

Speech therapy

Communication support is included among the institute’s therapy services.

03

Cultural and religious activities

The existing program includes cultural and religious activities, described on the original site as cultural and religious therapy.

04

Vocational learning

Practical skills activities are included in the institute’s developmental programs.

05

Agricultural activities

Agro therapy is listed among the institute’s activities and approaches.

06

Study visits and excursions

Trips and shared experiences offer opportunities for learning beyond the institute.

07

Domestic life skills

Everyday practical learning supports participation in daily routines.

08

Celebrations and community participation

Religious, national and international occasions bring children together through events and activities.

Children and families

Care for a child.
Support for a family.

Our objectives recognise the wellbeing of both children and their parents. We aim to provide a supportive environment where children receive care and families can connect with the institute’s team.
